CompanyHunter

Welcome to CompanyHunter! Use the search form below to find companies based on location and main business line.

MainBusinessLine ID's can be found at https://stat.fi/fi/luokitukset/toimiala/

Note: Main Business Line IDs are required for searching. And if it results in 0 companies, you probably entered an invalid ID.

Note: Data will be saved to your browser's local storage.

Data attribution and licensing

This project uses open data from the Finnish Business Information System (YTJ), provided by the Finnish Patent and Registration Office (PRH) and the Finnish Tax Administration.

  • Source: PRH / YTJ Open Data
  • License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)

The source data has been trimmed and modified to make it lighter to process and easier to search.
